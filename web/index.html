A simulation of general & acute (G&A) hospital beds in England. Change any setting on the left and the model re-runs a year of simulated patients in your browser. Results are compared with a 2025/26 baseline calibrated to published NHS data. Every assumption is explained on the Methods page, and every input is listed with its source on Data & sources.
