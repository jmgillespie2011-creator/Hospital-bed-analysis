A simulation of general & acute (G&A) hospital beds in England. Change any setting and the model re-runs a year of simulated patients in your browser. Results are compared with a 2025/26 baseline calibrated to published NHS data. Every assumption is explained on the Methods page, and every input is listed with its source on Data & sources.

Average length of stay (days)

Published (HES)This scenario

Mean nights per hospital episode with at least one night, all ages and specialties (HES). A stay under several consultants counts as several episodes, so this is lower than the average stay per admission. The model does not measure it directly: the scenario point is the latest HES value scaled by the simulated change in nights per emergency admission.

Share of occupied G&A beds, December to February (winter daily sitreps; not collected in 2020/21 and 2021/22). The scenario value is the simulated year-round share at 08:00, the same measure the model is fitted to.
